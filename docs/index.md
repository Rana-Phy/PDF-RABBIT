# PDF-RABBIT

An end-to-end analysis framework for X-ray total scattering data, with a fully
integrated workflow of physically grounded corrections. PDF-RABBIT extracts
reliable, reproducible `S(Q)` and `G(r)` functions by optimizing hyperparameters
under physically meaningful boundary conditions.

Developed in the Laboratory of Structural Inorganic Chemistry, Faculty of
Engineering, Hokkaido University, under the supervision of Professor Akira Miura.
